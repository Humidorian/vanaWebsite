An indigenous (Scheduled Tribe)–owned enterprise rooted in the Eastern Ghats along the River Sileru, Mothugudem, Telangana — delivering clean energy built by the community, for the community.

Who we are
Indigenous-owned & women-led. Promoted by Ms Veena Soyam and powered by the women of the Koya Scheduled Tribe, we turn the sun over our own ancestral lands into affordable, reliable electricity.
What we do
Solar EPC & Energy-as-a-Service. We design, build and maintain rooftop and utility-scale solar — and finance it through a pay-as-you-use service model so green power is within reach for every home and business.

What we deliver

From a single rooftop to community-scale arrays, our end-to-end solar capability spans design, engineering, procurement, construction and lifelong maintenance — with flexible financing that removes the upfront barrier.

Commercial & industrial

Utility-scale solar EPC

Large community and utility-scale arrays that generate meaningful power for the grid and the region.
