Who we are

Vana Aishwarya — clean power from the heart of the forest

Owned by the Koya Scheduled Tribe community of the Eastern Ghats and led by women, Vana Aishwarya Green Enterprises is a genuinely indigenous (Scheduled Tribe) enterprise — and that distinction carries real weight: priority access to funding, easier loans, and strong ESG value for every business we partner with. We are an engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) company and an Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS) partner, delivering clean energy across residential, commercial and industrial projects.

Power Purchase Agreement

Go solar with ₹0 investment

Under Energy-as-a-Service, Vana Aishwarya establishes the solar system at your premises and supplies the power you use on mutually beneficial terms — no capital outlay, immediate savings.

  • Zero capital, immediate savings

    No upfront cost, no loans, no EMIs. Your power costs drop the day the plant is commissioned.

  • Fixed tariff, no tariff hikes

    Lock in a rate 20–40% below your current grid tariff — insulated from rising electricity prices for 25 years.

  • We carry the performance risk

    Generation, maintenance and performance are on us — you only pay for the power you actually use.

  • Full monitoring & maintenance

    24×7 remote monitoring and scheduled maintenance — all included, so you never lift a finger.
